SyncAboutIt

A personal notes app for shopping lists, project notes, ideas, and sourced web clips. Notes save on your device and sync through your own Google Drive account. Optional folders organize projects; labels work across folders.

SyncAboutIt includes a browser app, a Chromium extension for clipping web content, an optional AI-agent connector (MCP) for note-taking assistants, and a native Android client.

This is a personal-use application, not a public product or service.
